Privacy Policy for SwiftShare

Last updated: October 8, 2026

SwiftShare is an Android application for sharing files and other content between devices. This Privacy Policy explains how SwiftShare handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Collect

SwiftShare is designed to operate without requiring an online account or a backend server. Based on the current app design, we do not intentionally collect, sell, or store personal information on our servers.

The app may access files, photos, videos, or other content that you choose to share. Such access is used only to provide the app's sharing functionality.

2. Device and Local Network Access

Depending on the features you use and the Android version of your device, SwiftShare may request permissions needed to select files or communicate with another device for sharing. Information handled for these features is used to provide the requested functionality and is not intentionally sent to a SwiftShare backend server.

3. How We Use Information

Information or content you select is used only to perform the sharing operation you initiate. We do not use your files or personal information for advertising, profiling, or sale.

4. Sharing of Information

SwiftShare does not intentionally share your personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. Content is shared with another device only when you initiate a sharing operation.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the app's design to protect information handled by the app. You should only share content with devices and people you trust.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

SwiftShare does not maintain a server-side account or personal-data database. Content handled locally by the app remains under your control on your device. You can delete files created or received by the app using the normal file-management features of your device.

7. Children's Privacy

SwiftShare is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third-Party Services

If a future version of SwiftShare adds third-party SDKs or services that collect or process user data, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety information will be updated before those features are used.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the SwiftShare Google Play Store listing.
